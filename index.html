1. 数据本地化
生煎健身 (SJ Fitness) 奉行“数据私有”原则。您的所有训练动作、重量组数及身体指标优先存储于您的设备本地。我们不设置强制性的云端账户，数据完全由您掌控。

2. 核心功能与权限
• 自定义动作库：仅存储于本地，用于构建您的私人训练模版。
• HealthKit (Apple Health)：经您授权后，我们仅读取/写入活动数据。我们严禁将任何健康数据用于广告、营销或出售给第三方。

3. 用户权利
您可以随时在 App 内清除所有缓存或导出数据。我们不运行任何后台追踪程序，也不收集您的身份信息。

🇺🇸 English

1. Local-First Data
SJ Fitness follows a "Data Privacy" philosophy. All your exercises, weights, and metrics are stored locally on your device. We do not require a cloud account; your data stays with you.

2. Features & Permissions
• Custom Library: Stored locally to build your personalized training templates.
• HealthKit (Apple Health): With your consent, we read/write activity data. We strictly prohibit using HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or selling to third parties.

3. User Control
You can clear all cached data or export your logs directly within the app at any time. We do not use background trackers or collect identity information.
